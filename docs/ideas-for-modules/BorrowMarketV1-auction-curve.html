Auction price curve

One curve for challenges and forced sales: it starts at 10x the position price and ends at 0 at time T. The shaded band below the dashed line is the 1x level, where the sale clears under the position price.

n = 2 (recommended) n = 3

Formula

price(t) = 10 x positionPrice x (1 - t/T)^n

remaining = T - elapsed
price = mulDiv(mulDiv(start, remaining, T), remaining, T)

Price as a multiple of the position price
